THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Prioritise organisational change as the reason to approach an enterprise account.

Before outreach

How the market was prioritised.

  1. 01

    Signals used

    Mass hiring, leadership change, restructuring, L&D investment, enterprise growth and large teams needing faster knowledge transfer.

  2. 02

    Account logic

    Select 10,000-plus employee organisations where organisational change made learning enablement a board-level operational problem.

  3. 03

    Outreach angle

    Give senior L&D and enablement buyers a reason to talk that is tied to change inside their organisation.

During the campaign

How the campaign was run.

  1. 04

    Segment enterprise accounts

    Prioritise organisations where the potential value justified a more researched approach.

  2. 05

    Identify change signals

    Use mass hiring, leadership changes and restructuring as indicators of a current L&D need.

  3. 06

    Map senior stakeholders

    Reach the executives and VPs responsible for learning, enablement and organisational capability.

  4. 07

    Coordinate with the team

    Run a separate enterprise campaign alongside the internal SDR team.
