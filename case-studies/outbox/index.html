THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Turn outreach into a managed learning and conversion system.

SalesSourcers analysed the offer, prioritised relevant niches and decision-makers, then created sequences around the practical outcomes AI chat and voice agents could deliver.

01

Select the niches

Identify segments where AI agents could directly improve support, response speed or bookings.

02

Clarify the message

Connect the product to a recognisable operational problem rather than leading with generic AI claims.

03

Coordinate channels

Use email, calling and structured follow-up to progress interest into qualified demos.

04

Learn from response

Use replies and live conversations to refine the product narrative and target market.

SIGNAL STRATEGY

How the market was prioritised before outreach started.

01

Signals used

Service-heavy companies with slow response times, high inbound enquiry volume, manual booking workflows and visible customer support bottlenecks.

02

Account logic

Segment by operational pain where AI voice or chat agents could produce a practical commercial outcome within weeks.

03

Outreach angle

Avoid broad AI language. Tie the message to response speed, booking efficiency or support coverage, then test which niche produced the clearest buying intent.
