THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Turn the ideal client profile into a signal-led, measurable campaign.

The campaign focused on smaller businesses ready to invest in marketing, then prioritised signals such as rapid staff growth and paid-search activity without corresponding organic visibility.

01

Define the market

Focus on businesses with the size, growth stage and willingness to invest in marketing.

02

Find visible signals

Prioritise staff growth and advertising behaviour that indicated current acquisition pressure.

03

Personalise the reason

Connect each message to a specific gap or growth event rather than a broad agency introduction.

04

Track every conversion

Use structured follow-up and CRM visibility to understand how outreach progressed.

SIGNAL STRATEGY

How the market was prioritised before outreach started.

01

Signals used

Staff growth, paid-search spend, weak organic visibility, inconsistent social presence and businesses investing in marketing without a clear strategy.

02

Account logic

Prioritise companies with visible acquisition pressure and enough growth ambition to value a strategic agency relationship.

03

Outreach angle

Reference the observable gap, show why it matters commercially, then qualify whether the buyer had the appetite to fix it now.
