THE COMMERCIAL SITUATION

A new service and new geography required evidence before the team could confidently invest.

FiveX wanted to help German companies expand through Bol.com while continuing to grow in the Netherlands, but lacked the bandwidth to research accounts, test pain points and sustain outreach.

01

Growth relied on referrals and small-scale outreach, with no repeatable system behind it.

02

The team needed to validate a new offer for German companies entering the Dutch market.

03

Different regions and buyer situations required separate messages and propositions.

THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Run two clear market tests instead of forcing one campaign to answer every question.

Before outreach

How the market was prioritised.

  1. 01

    Signals used

    Cross-border expansion, marketplace presence, Bol.com activity, ecommerce growth and brands showing operational pressure in new regions.

  2. 02

    Account logic

    Separate German, Polish and Dutch market hypotheses so each segment had its own message, list and response benchmark.

  3. 03

    Outreach angle

    Use expansion or marketplace friction as the reason to engage, then compare which region and pain converted into meetings fastest.

During the campaign

How the campaign was run.

  1. 04

    Separate the markets

    Create distinct campaigns for cross-border expansion and domestic marketplace performance.

  2. 05

    Prioritise growth signals

    Find companies showing the scale and readiness to expand into the Netherlands.

  3. 06

    Test the pain points

    Compare messages across regions and buyer situations to identify what created response.

  4. 07

    Manage execution

    Run the data, messaging, deliverability and calling, and keep improving all of it.
