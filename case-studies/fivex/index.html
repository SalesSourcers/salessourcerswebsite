THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Run two clear market tests instead of forcing one campaign to answer every question.

One motion targeted German and Polish companies showing expansion signals. The second focused on established Dutch eCommerce teams with a need for stronger Bol.com management.

01

Separate the markets

Create distinct campaigns for cross-border expansion and domestic marketplace performance.

02

Prioritise growth signals

Find companies showing the scale and readiness to expand into the Netherlands.

03

Test the pain points

Compare messages across regions and buyer situations to identify what created response.

04

Manage execution

Own data, messaging, deliverability, calling and continuous optimisation end to end.
