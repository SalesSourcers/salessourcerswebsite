THE COMMERCIAL SITUATION

The channel had been written off, but the real failure was a lack of precision.

Cypago needed access to CISOs, Heads of Cybersecurity and other senior security leaders. Broad activity and generic outreach could not earn attention in a technically demanding market.

01

The in-house SDR team had not delivered enough qualified opportunities and was shut down.

02

Three outsourced agencies had also failed, leaving outbound activity close to non-existent.

03

The company needed senior security conversations, not a larger volume of loosely qualified meetings.

THE CAMPAIGN DESIGN

Treat cybersecurity credibility and account selection as prerequisites, not campaign extras.

SalesSourcers used specialist CyberTech knowledge to refine the market, train the SDR and focus execution on senior buyers with a credible reason to engage.

01

Narrow the ICP

Prioritise CISOs, Heads of Cybersecurity and relevant GRC leaders inside suitable accounts.

02

Build technical fluency

Train the SDR on the product, security context, common objections and buyer priorities.

03

Prioritise quality

Use fit, role and timing to decide where calling and personalised follow-up should concentrate.

04

Coach from calls

Use conversation feedback to sharpen the message and improve how the offer was positioned.

SIGNAL STRATEGY

How the market was prioritised before outreach started.

01

Signals used

Security leadership changes, compliance hiring, public GRC initiatives, audit pressure and companies with complex cloud or vendor-risk environments.

02

Account logic

Filter for senior cybersecurity ownership and organisations where compliance automation could create measurable operational savings.

03

Outreach angle

Open with the risk or workload signal, not a generic platform pitch, then qualify whether the buyer owned the compliance or security operations pain.
