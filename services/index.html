Four ways in. One motion underneath.

Most agencies sell these as separate products. They are the same programme described from different angles, and the one you should ask for depends on what you already have.

Which one do you need?

If you have nobody prospecting, you want an outsourced SDR. If you have closers but an empty calendar, you want appointment setting. If your buyers answer phones, you want cold calling. If you are not yet sure who to target, you want lead generation.

Underneath, all four run the same way: define the market, build the playbook, live in week three, coached three times a week, every call recorded.

The four, and what actually separates them

Outsourced SDR

A dedicated rep who works your accounts and nobody else's. You are buying the person and the management around them - recruitment, training, coaching and replacement all move to us. Choose this when the constraint is that nobody is prospecting and nobody has the hours to manage someone who does. Outsourced SDR services.

B2B appointment setting

The same work, bought by the outcome rather than the head. Choose this when you already have closers and the problem is a thin calendar. The thing to interrogate is the qualification bar, because a meeting number is the easiest metric in B2B to hit dishonestly. B2B appointment setting.

Cold calling

The channel rather than the arrangement. Choose this when your buyers are reachable by phone and email alone has stalled. Between 1,500 and 5,000 targeted calls a month, recorded, with the recordings available to you. Cold calling services.

B2B lead generation

The widest of the four, and the term most abused. Ours ends in a booked conversation, not a list or a form fill. Choose this when the market itself is still an open question and the definition work matters as much as the outreach. B2B lead generation.

What is identical across all four

The engagement does not change shape depending on which name you bought. Strategy and build in weeks one and two, live in week three, manager coaching three times a week, every call recorded with AI notes, and reporting broken down by role, region and message.

If you are comparing us with hiring instead, the loaded-cost comparison is the honest place to start. If you want to know what it costs, the pricing page explains the drivers rather than hiding behind a form.

What is the difference between outsourced SDR and appointment setting?+

Mostly what you are buying rather than what happens. Outsourced SDR gives you a dedicated person whose recruitment, training, coaching and replacement are our problem. Appointment setting buys the outcome instead. The work underneath is the same, so the question that matters in either case is the qualification bar.

Can we start with one and add another?+

There is nothing to add. All four descriptions cover one programme, so a campaign that starts as email-led appointment setting and becomes phone-heavy is a change of emphasis, not a new contract.

How quickly can we expect meetings?+

Most campaigns go live in week three, after strategy, infrastructure and SDR training are complete. Qualified meetings can begin from launch, and performance becomes more predictable as real market feedback improves the campaign.

Will your SDR sound like our company?+

Before launch we interview your team, study your offer, review customer calls and materials, build the sales playbook and train your SDR on your market. Scripts are tested through role play, call listening and live coaching. Your team approves the positioning, and recordings plus email replies keep quality visible.

What visibility do we get?+

Full call visibility, recordings, AI-generated call notes and a live dashboard. You can listen to any conversation, see which messages convert by role and region, and track meetings from booked through to attended.
